Weather Plans

Two rule-based ways to own the Nasdaq's biggest companies.

Both plans read the same market weather each evening. When the market is calm they stay invested; when it turns stormy they move part of the money into a Treasury bill fund. No stock research, no news, no guessing.

QQQ Weather Plan

Hold the whole Nasdaq-100 through one fund, QQQ. The Standard version adds a 2x fund in calm markets; the Simple version never goes above 100%.

  • Longest test: since 1999, through the dot-com crash and 2008.
  • One or two funds. The easiest plan to follow.
  • Standard trades about 26 times a year and uses a 2x fund when calm.

Big Eight Weather Plan

Own only the eight largest Nasdaq-100 companies, sized by company value and refreshed each quarter. Never uses borrowed money or 2x funds.

  • Highest growth since 2011, when the giants led the market.
  • About 8 weather trades a year plus a quarterly line-up check.
  • Only 15 years of history. A bet that the biggest keep leading.
